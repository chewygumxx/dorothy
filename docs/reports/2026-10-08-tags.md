# Tags probe

Four live chats, driven in tmux against the real model, check that Dorothy
coins and reuses concepts in her reviews and that she lists and searches by
them. They also check that a rename through `--edit-tags` holds, and that a
`--memory` save made while a review runs keeps the tags the review added.
Every step behaved as the plan expected.

- Spec: [`docs/specs/2026-10-07-tags-design.md`](../specs/2026-10-07-tags-design.md)
- Plan: [`docs/plans/2026-10-08-tags.md`](../plans/2026-10-08-tags.md),
  Task 14
- Branch: `feat/tags` at `6a9b15b`
- Sample data: [`2026-10-08-tags/`](2026-10-08-tags/), described under
  [Sample data](#sample-data)

## Setup

Dorothy ran as `bun src/index.ts` in a 120 by 40 tmux session, on
`claude-sonnet-5-5`. `XDG_DATA_HOME`, `XDG_CACHE_HOME` and
`XDG_CONFIG_HOME` were all under one temporary directory, so no real
transcripts, sidecars or `tags.json` were read or written.
`DOTENVX_CONFIG=~/.config/dotenvx` let dotenvx find its key, as in the
compaction probe. `config.toml` held one setting:

```toml
[memory]
idle-seconds = 10
```

With a 10-second idle, a review ran after nearly every exchange rather than
once per chat. Messages were typed with `tmux send-keys`. The two editor
steps used small scripts as `$VISUAL`, which save a copy of the view and
then change it with `sed`.

The probe cost about $0.31: $0.10 for the chats and $0.21 for the reviews,
summed from the status lines and each sidecar's `reviewCostUsd`.

## Coining and reuse

**Run.** Three chats, each quit after its review:

1. Reviving a sluggish sourdough starter: feeding ratio, an acetone smell,
   keeping it in the fridge.
2. A bicycle's gears skipping: where to look, a chain at 0.75% wear,
   setting the barrel adjuster.
3. Back to sourdough: telling when the starter is at peak, and a dense,
   gummy loaf.

**Expected.** Concepts for both subjects, each with a scope note, and the
third chat reusing the first chat's concepts rather than coining
near-duplicates.

**Happened.** The first chat's first review coined three concepts. The
`tags.json` that review wrote was at `rev` 1:

```json
"k968b3535": {
  "prefLabel": "sourdough starter",
  "broader": ["k9a718830"],
  "scopeNote": "Maintaining, reviving and troubleshooting sourdough starters, including feeding schedules, ratios, smells and signs of spoilage."
},
"k9a718830": {
  "prefLabel": "sourdough baking",
  "broader": [],
  "scopeNote": "Baking bread with wild-yeast sourdough cultures, including starter care and readiness for baking."
},
"k7dc0289a": {
  "prefLabel": "fermentation troubleshooting",
  "broader": [],
  "scopeNote": "Diagnosing problems in fermented foods and cultures, such as odd smells, sluggish activity, hooch or mold."
}
```

The second chat coined three more, at `rev` 2: `bike maintenance`
(`k459bcd6a`, with the alternative label `bicycle repair`), plus
`drivetrain troubleshooting` and `derailleur adjustment`, both placed under
it. Each had a one-sentence scope note.

The third chat coined nothing: `tags.json` stayed at `rev` 2 with the same
six concepts. Its sidecar carried the first chat's three ids, in the same
order (trimmed to the fields that matter here):

```json
{
  "title": "Sourdough starter peak and a dense, gummy loaf",
  "tags": ["k968b3535", "k9a718830", "k7dc0289a"],
  "fields": { "tags": { "by": "dorothy" } },
  "reviewedThrough": 4
}
```

`dorothy --tags` then printed:

```text
bike maintenance (1)
  derailleur adjustment (1)
  drivetrain troubleshooting (1)
fermentation troubleshooting (2)
sourdough baking (2)
  sourdough starter (2)
```

## Listing and searching

**Run.** A fourth chat asked "What topics have our past chats covered? Look
them up rather than guess.", then "Find the chats tagged with sourdough
starter, using the tag, and tell me their titles."

**Expected.** The dim lines `⌕ listed tags · <n> tags` and
`⌕ searched tagged <label> · <n> conversations`, and answers naming the
topics and the chats.

**Happened.**

```text
         ⌕ listed tags · 6 tags
dorothy  I checked my tags, and our past chats fall into two areas:
         1. Sourdough baking
         ...
         2. Bike maintenance
         ...
         The tags show three conversations in all, two on sourdough and one
         on bike repair.
```

```text
         ⌕ searched tagged sourdough starter · 2 conversations
dorothy  Two chats carry the "sourdough starter" tag:
         1. Reviving a sluggish sourdough starter
         2. Sourdough starter peak and a dense, gummy loaf
```

The transcript recorded both lookups as `recall` events (trimmed of `v`,
`at`, `id` and `offset`):

```json
{"kind":"recall","ok":true,"tool":"tags","hits":6}
{"kind":"recall","ok":true,"tool":"search","query":"","hits":2,
 "tags":["sourdough starter"]}
```

## A rename holds

**Run.** `dorothy --edit-tags` with an editor script that changed
`Tag: sourdough starter` to `Tag: levain`. After that, `--resume` on the
third chat, one more sourdough message (shortening the bulk ferment), and
an idle past its review.

**Expected.** The new label stands in `tags.json` with the same id, the
third chat still carries it, and no concept with the old label is coined
again.

**Happened.** The view showed one block per concept in label order, with
its provenance and count:

```text
Concept: k968b3535
Tag: sourdough starter
Also:
Under: sourdough baking
Note:
Maintaining, reviving and troubleshooting sourdough starters, including
feeding schedules, ratios, smells and signs of spoilage.
# (Dorothy, claude-sonnet-5-5, 2026-10-07; 3 conversations)
```

After the save, `tags.json` went to `rev` 3, and `k968b3535` read
`prefLabel: "levain"` with `edited` stamped. The resumed chat's review
retitled it ("Sourdough starter peak, dense loaf and shorter bulk") and
kept `["k968b3535", "k9a718830", "k7dc0289a"]`. `tags.json` stayed at
`rev` 3, and no concept was labelled `sourdough starter`.

An earlier try opened Neovim, because `$VISUAL` is set in this shell and
outranks `$EDITOR`. Quitting it without saving left `tags.json` untouched,
and the command exited 0.

## Editing a chat while its review runs

This step checks the fix in `01c6aec`. Before that fix, a `--memory` save
pruned the chat's tags against a vocabulary read before the editor
opened.

**Run.** In a second tmux window, `--memory` on the third chat, with an
editor script that waited 50 seconds and then changed only
`Pinned: no` to `Pinned: yes`. While it waited, the chat window got an
unrelated question (how often to descale an espresso machine), and the
review ran.

**Expected.** The review coins a concept and tags the chat with it. The
`--memory` save then keeps that tag, with Dorothy's provenance, beside
the pin.

**Happened.** Times are local (AEDT); the files' stamps are in UTC.

- 05:15:51: the editor opened.
- 05:16:23: the review wrote `tags.json` at `rev` 4, coining
  `espresso machine care` (`kb25919c9`), and tagged the chat.
- 05:16:41: the editor saved, and `--memory` exited 0.

The sidecar after the save (trimmed):

```json
{
  "tags": ["k968b3535", "k9a718830", "k7dc0289a", "kb25919c9"],
  "fields": {
    "tags": {
      "by": "dorothy",
      "at": "2026-10-07T18:16:23.795Z",
      "model": "claude-sonnet-5-5",
      "throughTurn": 8
    }
  },
  "pinned": true,
  "reviewedThrough": 8
}
```

The concept coined while the editor was open survived the save, and the
set is still Dorothy's. The final tree:

```text
bike maintenance (2)
  derailleur adjustment (1)
  drivetrain troubleshooting (2)
espresso machine care (1)
fermentation troubleshooting (2)
sourdough baking (3)
  levain (3)
```

## Findings

None is a defect.

- **A recap chat carries the topics it recalls.** The fourth chat, which
  only asked about the others, was tagged `levain`, `sourdough baking`,
  `bike maintenance` and `drivetrain troubleshooting`. So the
  `bike maintenance` count rose from 1 to 2 in the final tree. The spec
  leaves what a chat is about to her review, and the recap did discuss
  those topics. Still, counts can overstate how often a subject came up.
- **Reviews cost about twice the chats.** At a 10-second idle she reviewed
  after nearly every exchange. At the default idle there are fewer
  reviews.
- **She coins hierarchy unprompted.** Both subjects got a broader concept
  with narrower ones under it, and she added an alternative label
  (`bicycle repair`) without being asked.

## Sample data

[`2026-10-08-tags/`](2026-10-08-tags/) holds what the probe left behind.
It is a real data directory: with `XDG_DATA_HOME` pointed at a copy of its
`data/`, `dorothy --tags` and `--list` read it back as shown above.

- `data/dorothy/tags.json`: the vocabulary at the end, `rev` 4.
- `data/dorothy/transcripts/`: the four chats' transcripts (`.jsonl`) and
  sidecars (`.meta.json`). Phrases: `vociferate-ten-presider-expunging`
  (starter), `grandson-cabob-vasectomizing-propagate` (gears),
  `anabolic-gat-sangh-autodidact` (back to sourdough, then espresso) and
  `clammed-reversionary-chick-pulverize` (the recap).
- `snapshots/`: `tags.json` after the first, second and third chats, and
  after the rename.
- `views/`: the `--edit-tags` view before the rename, and the `--memory`
  view as opened and as saved in the last step.
- `probe/`: the environment, `config.toml`, the send-and-wait helper and
  the two editor scripts.

The JSON files were reformatted by Biome to the repository's indentation,
since the repository's format check covers them; their content is as
written. `views/` holds `.txt` copies of the editor's Markdown, so that the
Markdown lint, which would hold them to this repository's style, skips
them.
