# Abstract (Dorothy, claude-sonnet-5-5, 2026-10-07)
Abstract:
The user's sourdough starter is now doubling reliably, first in six
hours and by the end in five hours at 26°C, so the revival is working.
They asked briefly how to spot peak. Dorothy listed a domed top just
starting to dimple (concave means past peak), many bubbles, a sweet
tangy smell, a light jiggly stringy texture, and the float test (not
foolproof), and suggested a rubber band at the starting level. The last
loaf was dense and gummy; Dorothy said it was most likely underproofed,
probably because the starter was still weak. She advised using it at
peak and judging bulk by the dough (up 50-75%, domed, jiggly, bubbly),
at 24-27°C. Asked whether to shorten bulk, she said probably yes: at
26°C with about 20% starter, bulk often takes 4-6 hours, so start
checking at 3.5-4 hours. Less starter (10%) means longer. The user keeps
asking for brief answers. Open: whether the next loaf improves, and
whether the acetone smell faded.
